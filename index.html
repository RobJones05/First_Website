Zamalek review

Zamalek is the popular South African township nickname for Carling Black Label beer, a strong pale lager with 5.5% alcohol by volume.

Origin of the Name

1993 Football Match:

The nickname started after a fierce 1993 African Champions Cup clash between the South African club Kaizer Chiefs and the Egyptian powerhouse Zamalek SC.Tough Reputation: After Zamalek beat Chiefs in a tight match, local fans drinking the full-bodied Black Label in taverns joked that the beer was as tough and strong as the Egyptian team.Matching Colors: The black, white, and red label of the beer also happened to match the official colors of the Zamalek football club

How it is Served

The Quart:

It is most famously bought and sold as a 750ml large glass bottle, known locally as a "quart".Shared Drink: The big bottle is traditionally served very cold and meant to be shared with friends at a braai or local shebeen
